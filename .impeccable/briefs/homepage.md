# Surface Brief — Homepage (Landing)

## Scope & Mode
- **Surface:** Homepage / Landing page
- **Mode:** Experience — the work itself leads from the first viewport
- **Platform:** Web (responsive, mobile-first)

## Audience, Job, Action
- **Audience:** Mixed — tech recruiters, potential clients, fellow developers
- **Job:** Evaluate Elvien's engineering capability in under 60 seconds
- **Action:** Explore the collection (navigate to projects, about, contact)
- **Proof:** Live deployed projects, real GitHub activity, verifiable certificates

## Constraints
- Light + dark mode (next-themes)
- English copywriting, catchy
- Multi-page structure (not single-page scroll)
- Modern minimalist + playful professional animations
- Mobile-first responsive

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a museum vitrine where each project is a preserved specimen — classified, labeled, and displayed under glass with the reverence a natural history collection gives its rarest finds. The category default (a grid of project cards with hover effects and a hero tagline) is refused. The visitor enters a collection, and the interface itself is the taxonomy system. "Live proof over claims" is literal: each specimen links to a living demo. The clinical precision is the personality.

OWN-WORLD: Restrained palette with committed fields — off-white linen ground with deep ink, brass owning full rectangular surfaces on labels and interactive elements, herbarium green as solid-field status indicators. Glass vitrine borders (1px + inner shadow), pin-mounted thumbnails at 2° tilt, specimen label cards with ruled fields and miniature architecture diagrams, brass drawer-tab navigation. Workhorse serif (small caps) paired with tight-tracked sans for metadata. Recognizable with all content removed by: the brass field commitments, the glass borders, the tilted pin-mount shadows, the linen/felt texture, and the drawer-slide motion.

STORY: The visitor enters what feels like a curated museum gallery. They see a featured specimen immediately — the strongest project, large, with its full classification label. They understand this is an engineer who builds and ships real systems. They scan the collection counter ("12 specimens · 8 live"), read the data ticker, and know this is a living collection. They navigate via brass drawer tabs to explore the full collection, read field notes about the researcher, verify credentials, or check the laboratory dashboard. They leave knowing exactly what Elvien builds, that it works, and how to reach him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width vitrine case. Top-left: brass museum plate "ELVIEN — FULL-STACK SPECIMENS" in tracked small caps with collection counter in small monospace. Below: horizontal ruled line. Center-mass: one featured specimen displayed large — pin-mounted screenshot (2° tilt) with full specimen label card beside it (name, classification, year, stack taxonomy with universal glyphs, status LIVE on solid green field, miniature architecture diagram, micro-sparkline, recent commit in monospace). Label's "View Living Specimen →" link in solid brass. Below: row of smaller specimen thumbnails with dotted connector lines between related projects. Navigation top-right as brass drawer tabs. Data ticker at viewport bottom.

FORM: The Specimen Cabinet, position 3 of 7 on the resonance-ordered list. Seed key a8b125b9.

FINISH: Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
