About Me

Background, work philosophy, and education.

I am a full-stack engineer who enjoys turning complex workflows into fast, clean software. I specialize in the modern React and Next.js ecosystem, writing strict TypeScript contracts from database schemas down to user interface components.

My work spans diverse problems: from building real-time cashier systems connecting to Bluetooth thermal hardware, to cloud automation bots bypassing strict government firewall proxies, to pixel-precise PDF generators for physical QR cards.

When building software, I focus on three core principles:

  • Live Proof: Working code in production always beats unfinished mockups.
  • Clean Architecture: Strict separation between business logic and UI components.
  • Speed & Accessibility: Fast loading times, keyboard navigation, and mobile-friendly tap targets.
